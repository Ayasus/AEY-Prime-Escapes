Our Story

AEY Prime Escapes was founded in 2009 by Aida Evaristo-Yuseco, a veteran real estate broker with a vision to create an agency that treated every client as family. Starting with a small team of five agents in Makati, AEY quickly grew through word-of-mouth built on honest service and genuine results.

By 2015, we had expanded to Cebu and Davao, establishing regional hubs that gave us true nationwide reach while maintaining the intimate, personal service that set us apart. Today, our 48-strong team of licensed brokers and specialists collectively manages over 1,200 active listings across all major Philippine property markets.

Our name carries meaning. "Prime" reflects our commitment to quality over quantity. "Escapes" reflects our belief that a home is not merely shelter — it is a sanctuary, a retreat, a place to exhale. We help families find not just a property, but their escape.
